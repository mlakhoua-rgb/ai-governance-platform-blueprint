## What this repository is

This repository is a **sanitized reference architecture** — an educational showcase of AI governance patterns for engineering mission-critical, financially sensitive platforms. It exists to demonstrate *how* such systems can be governed, not to disclose *what* any specific system does.

## What has been intentionally withheld

The following are deliberately **not** published here and have been replaced with generic equivalents, anonymized, or omitted entirely:

- **Product names** — all platform, service, and internal project names are fictionalized
- **Vendor and tool choices** — specific commercial products, cloud services, and proprietary tooling are referred to by category ("a message broker", "a CI system") rather than by name, even where the underlying choice is a well-known open-source project
- **Source code** — no proprietary production code is included; snippets are illustrative pseudocode written for this repository
- **Infrastructure configurations** — real deployment configs, network topologies, and environment details are withheld
- **Proprietary algorithms & thresholds** — trading, forecasting, risk-scoring, and alerting logic, parameters, and thresholds are not disclosed
- **Data schemas & business rules** — real data models, market logic, and domain-specific rules are abstracted
- **Security internals** — specific secret-management setups, access-control matrices, and defensive configurations are described at pattern level only
